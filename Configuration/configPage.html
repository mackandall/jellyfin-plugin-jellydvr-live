Live TV buffer

While someone watches a channel, the programme on it is recorded from the moment they tuned in, as a normal recording sharing their tuner stream. Pausing stays on the live stream; the buffer is for rewinding past tune-in, carrying on from another device, skipping commercials, or keeping the programme. Buffers are tagged "Live buffer" until kept, and deleted once nobody has watched them for a while.
Surfing past a channel starts nothing, so channel surfing doesn't tie up tuners. The buffer begins this long after you tuned in.
For apps that vanish without saying goodbye (network drop, crash, TV switched off), in case they come back.
When they're all in use, buffers on channels nobody is watching stop at once so a new channel or a scheduled recording can open. Kept buffers and anything being watched are never stopped.
